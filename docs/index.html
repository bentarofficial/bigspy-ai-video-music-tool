Bigspy AI Music Video Studio

Bigspy AI Music Video Studio is a Windows desktop app that turns your songs into music and lyric videos. It transcribes lyrics, matches background footage, adds animated subtitles and renders vertical videos ready for TikTok.

Features

  • AI lyric transcription and timed, animated subtitles
  • Automatic background footage and transitions
  • Built-in timeline editor
  • Connect your TikTok account and send finished videos to your TikTok inbox as drafts, then finish and post them in the TikTok app
