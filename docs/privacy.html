Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains how Bigspy AI Music Video Studio (the "App") handles your information. The App is a desktop application that runs on your own computer. Bigspy AI does not operate servers that collect or store your data.

1. Data stored on your computer

Your projects, media files and rendered videos stay on your computer. They are not sent to us.

2. TikTok data

If you choose to connect TikTok, you sign in on TikTok's own page (the App never sees your password) and grant these permissions:

  • user.info.basic: your TikTok display name and avatar, shown in the App so you know which account is connected.
  • video.upload: to send a video you selected to your TikTok inbox as a draft.

The access and refresh tokens TikTok issues are stored only on your computer and are used only to talk to TikTok's API. We do not sell, share or transfer TikTok data to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Data sent to third parties

The App only sends data where needed for a feature you use: the video file you choose to share is sent to TikTok; search keywords are sent to stock footage providers to find background clips.

4. Deleting your data

Disconnect TikTok in the App to delete stored tokens, or revoke the App's access in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. Uninstalling the App and deleting its folder removes all local data.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always available on this page.
